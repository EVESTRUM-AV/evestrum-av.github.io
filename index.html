I build rooms that listen back.

Art technologist and experimental musician, born 1984 in Italy, based in Berlin since 2014. My work sits between the patch and the plan: generative systems in TouchDesigner, granular and modular sound, and the unglamorous engineering that makes a space actually run — media servers, mapping, signal chains, load-in schedules.

Currently lead technician at Aura Labs, Berlin.

About

Emanuele Musca — Berlin

Photograph — Martina Giannetti

Emanuele Musca (b. 1984, Italy) is an art technologist and experimental ambient/electronic musician working in Berlin. Under the name Evestrum he builds otherworldly, haunting atmospheres out of modular synthesizer texture, long reverberation and deep sample research — meaningful sound deconstructed through granular synthesis. The project explores the interplay between sound and memory, in particular the recollections that surface in dreams or meditative states.

He graduated from the University of Siena in 2014 in Communication Studies, with a thesis on multimedia systems design — a sound device for children built around learning by doing, in Ableton Live, Arduino and Pure Data. He moved to Berlin shortly after.

Since 2015 he has worked in projection mapping and visual installation, with a long connection to the Genius Loci Weimar festival, where he served as media server operator across several editions, helping artists through the final stages of production and deployment onto the buildings. In 2016 he entered Berlin's experimental music scene through the Modern Bön collective.

In 2020, together with Vincenzo Gagliardi, he won the Genius Loci Weimar competition for a projection on the Marstall. Their piece, Inside, looked into the building's memory. He returned in 2021 as a member of the festival jury.

From 2021 he worked as a technician at The Lighthouse of Digital Art — now Aura Labs — alongside Francesco Della Toffola (xmtry_studio), a former Modern Bön member and long-running collaborator. Together they made the exhibition Latent Spaces (2022) and the live set Oblivion. The same year he worked on the planning and construction of KHROMA, a digital art centre in Berlin that opened in April 2023, including the build of its immersive playground room.

He is currently lead technician at Aura Labs on the RAW-Gelände in Berlin-Friedrichshain, where Beacon and Rite of Spring are on show.
