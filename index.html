MXWendler

2015 — 2021 · Berlin

Six years with the manufacturer. Specifying and assembling custom media servers up to twelve outputs — installing them on site, and training the operators who would run them.

Software testing, system integration and research, documentation for the company wiki and manuals, video tutorials, and IT administration. Customer support meant being the person on the phone when a server stopped in front of an audience.

  • BuildsMedia servers up to 12 outputs. Split-output architecture adopted after testing showed it outperformed single-card configurations
  • On siteInstallation, commissioning and operator training
  • SoftwareTesting, translation, wiki and manual authoring, video tutorials
  • ITSystem integration, hardware configuration, mail administration

About

Emanuele Musca — Berlin

Emanuele Musca (b. 1984, Italy) is an art technologist and experimental musician working in Berlin. He works in two directions: the technical side under his own name — exhibition planning, media servers, mapping, system integration — and artistic work under the name Evestrum.

As Evestrum he builds otherworldly, haunting atmospheres out of modular synthesizer texture, long reverberation and deep sample research, deconstructed through granular synthesis. The work explores sound and memory, in particular the recollections that surface in dreams or meditative states.

He graduated from the University of Siena in 2014 in Communication Studies, with a thesis on multimedia systems design — a sound device for children built around learning by doing, in Ableton Live, Arduino and Pure Data. He moved to Berlin shortly after.

From 2015 to 2021 he worked at MXWendler, planning, assembling and installing media servers, training operators and handling system integration — and running media servers at Genius Loci Weimar across six editions. In 2016 he entered Berlin's experimental music scene through the Modern Bön collective.

In 2020, with Vincenzo Gagliardi, he won the Genius Loci Weimar competition for a projection on the Marstall. Their piece, Inside, looked into the building's memory. He returned in 2021 on the jury.

Since 2021 he has been Lead technician for Lighthouse Arts GmbH, building out five venues in Berlin and Vienna. Alongside that he makes work with Francesco Della Toffola (xmtry_studio) — Latent Spaces, Oblivion, Beacon — and his own installations, Rite of Spring and the sound for Beacon.

In November 2026 he worked as assistant stage manager at Tehran Contemporary Sounds, silent green Kulturquartier, Berlin.
