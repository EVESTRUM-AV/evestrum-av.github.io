Year2026 — currently running
RoleBuild-up, planning, mapping, automation
CompanyLighthouse Arts GmbH
CityBerlin

The exhibition currently running in the former KHROMA Berlin space, and the largest mapping job in this list.

The venue is almost entirely projected — ninety projectors across the building, all of which I mapped. I worked on the build-up, planned two immersive rooms including one measuring fifteen by fifteen metres, configured the media servers and curated the automation for the whole venue.

At this scale the mapping is only half the job. The other half is making a building of that complexity start itself, run its day unattended, and be recoverable remotely when something drops off the network.
